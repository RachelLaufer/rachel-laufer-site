Rachel Laufer

Health Economics·Market Access·Global Health

Rachel is a health economics modeler and strategic consultant with more than a decade of experience spanning academic research and consulting, currently a Senior Consultant at Sia. Across both settings, her work has focused on accelerating access to care in low- and middle-income countries — from cost-effectiveness modeling of RSV prevention programs to cost-of-goods analyses that identify levers to lower pharmaceutical manufacturing costs.
